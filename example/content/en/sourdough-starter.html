How to make a sourdough starter in 7 days

A starter is flour and water left to ferment until wild yeast and bacteria take over. It needs a jar, a scale and a week of patience.

Day 1 to day 3

Mix 50 g of wholemeal flour with 50 g of water in a clean jar. Cover loosely and keep it at room temperature. Each day, discard half and feed it again with the same amounts.

Day 4 to day 7

Switch to white flour and feed twice a day. The starter is ready when it doubles within 4 to 8 hours of a feed and smells pleasantly sour.

Questions

Why is my starter not rising?

It is usually too cold. Keep it between 24 and 27 degrees Celsius for the first week.

Can I keep a starter in the fridge?

Yes. Feed it once a week and take it out the day before you bake.
