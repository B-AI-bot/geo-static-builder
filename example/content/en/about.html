About the bakery

Juniper & Rye was started by Sam Baker, a home baker who turned a weekend habit into a small shop. Sam and the team are fictional, like everything on this demo site.

Frequently asked questions

Is your bread really fermented for 24 hours?

Between 18 and 24 hours, depending on the temperature of the kitchen. The long fermentation builds flavour and keeps the crumb moist.

How many people join a bread class?

Up to 8 people, so everyone gets time at the bench.

Do I need any equipment for the class?

No. Bring an apron if you like; flour, bowls and a starter to take home are provided.
